Consortium Brief

An integrated upstream platform for oil & gas value creation

Mirage Global unites four complementary companies to identify, evaluate, acquire, finance, develop, operate and monetize oil and gas assets in Colombia and, potentially, international markets.

Main Objective Collaboration Model

01 Purpose of the consortium

Create an integrated upstream oil & gas platform

The consortium is established to identify, evaluate, acquire, finance, develop, operate and monetize oil and gas assets — combining sourcing, technical evaluation, capital structure, operations and monetization in one coordinated platform.

To create an integrated upstream oil and gas platform capable of sourcing petroleum opportunities, technically evaluating them, structuring and financing acquisitions, developing reserves and resources, and operating and monetizing producing and exploration assets.
